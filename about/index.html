Where I'm from

I grew up in Stonewall, Louisiana, a small town south of Shreveport. Now I'm in Ruston, studying Computer Science at Louisiana Tech in the Honors College, class of 2028.

02 — TRAINING

Training

I started lifting three years ago, and it stuck. It's a hobby for now, but I plan to step on stage and compete within the next couple of years. I've been obsessed with sports science and neurology for a while, and that got me into the gym in the first place.

Now that has spilled into code. Currently, I'm building a fitness app that follows your progress in the gym and outside it. It stays under wraps until launch.
