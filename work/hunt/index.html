01 — WHAT IT IS

What it is

A maintenance analytics dashboard for the mill. It helps plan preventive maintenance, flags equipment that behaves unusually, and shows how the planned work is getting done.

On the operations side, a separate monitor watches trim loss and raises alerts. Screens and data stay private, so this page describes the work in general terms.

02 — MY PART

My part

I'm one of two lead developers on the maintenance dashboard. With a small team, I help design and build the product: how it is laid out and how it runs at the mill. On the operations side, I contribute views and occasional coding to the trim-loss monitor.

03 — HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Mirrorwork-order data is copied, read-only, from the mill's system
  2. Refreshthe copy updates every hour
  3. Planpreventive maintenance is planned from past work orders
  4. Flagunusual equipment behavior is marked for a closer look
  5. Trackexecution health shows how planned work gets done
  6. Watchthe operations monitor raises trim-loss alerts
