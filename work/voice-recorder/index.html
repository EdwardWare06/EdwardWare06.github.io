Voice Recorder

A speaker-ID tool for small teams. It transcribes a room recording, splits it by speaker, and puts a name on every line. Everything runs on one machine.

01 — WHAT IT IS

What it is

Import a room recording, or record one in the browser. The app transcribes it, works out who spoke when, and matches each voice against the team's enrolled voiceprints.

When someone says "My name is…", it links that name to the voice. Open any transcript to confirm or correct a speaker; every confirmation improves the next match. Audio, transcripts and voiceprints never leave the machine.

02 — HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. Uploada recording, or live audio from the browser over a WebSocket
  2. Normalizeffmpeg converts it to 16 kHz mono
  3. Transcribefaster-whisper
  4. Diarizepyannote 3.1 works out who spoke when
  5. Embedan ECAPA voiceprint for every line
  6. Matchcosine similarity against enrolled members
  7. Name link"My name is…" ties a name to a voice
  8. Storetranscript, speakers and audio stay local
